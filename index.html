Work

  1. Garment folding from model-written programs 2026

    Gemini writes the folding program, a two-arm UR5e cell in MuJoCo runs it, and 31 of 77 long-sleeve tops come out folded. The last stubborn crease was the cloth mesh's fault, not the robot's.

  2. One cell, three simulators 2026

    The same two arms described once and run in MuJoCo, Gazebo and Isaac Sim, with 45 checks that they agree. The first agreement I reported was wrong, and the write-up says why.

  3. Robot foundation model 2026

    My own VLA and world action model, neither trained to a result yet. The pi0.5 baseline gets 93 of 100 on a simulated lift.

  4. Residual RL for a scripted handoff 2026

    A small PPO correction on top of a scripted two-arm handoff, from 25% success to between 85% and 100%, in simulation.

  5. Navigation stack from scratch 2026

    Five planners and five controllers for a TurtleBot4, no Nav2 behaviour tree. The C++ A* runs 102 to 193 times faster than the Python one. Video, try it.

  6. Replanning on a UR12e 2026

    Pick and place that replans around things moving into its path, and admits it cannot see anything within 11 cm of the arm. Try it.

  7. Quadruped navigation cost 2026

    Four cost functions for a Unitree Go2 on 120 courses. They all tie once a MuJoCo check fixed a constant I had made up. Try it.

  8. Swerve bases for an arena match 2026

    Two swerve-drive bases running an arena rulebook's match in MuJoCo on the navigation stack's planners, within about 6 mm of the path.

  9. LeHome garment challenge 2026

    ACT, Diffusion Policy and smolVLA on four garment types in Isaac Sim. 60.4% overall, 78% on long tops.

  10. Custom DWA local planner 2025

    The dynamic window approach from scratch for a TurtleBot3. Somehow my most-starred repo. Video.

  11. K.A.L.B 2023 to 2025

    A twelve-axis quadruped walking and mapping in Gazebo. Video.

A few of these run in your browser, on Python pulled straight from the repos: demos. The rest is on GitHub, and the datasets are on Hugging Face, private for now.
